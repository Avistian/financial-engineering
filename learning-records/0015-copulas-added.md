# Copulas added to Year 3 Q1

Added two planned units on copulas to the curriculum, placed in **Year 3 Q1
(portfolio construction & risk)** — after covariance cleaning / signal combination and
before HRP, costs, and VaR/ES:

- **085 Copulas I** — Sklar's theorem, margins vs dependence, Gaussian & Student-t,
  Spearman/Kendall (dependence beyond linear ρ).
- **086 Copulas II** — tail dependence, Archimedean families, simulation for risk;
  Gaussian-copula crash failure mode (Li 2000 as cautionary case).

**Why here, not Y1 Q3:** Unit 029 already teaches multivariate covariance (DCC/EWMA).
Copulas earn their keep when building portfolios and stressing joint losses — and
McNeil–Frey–Embrechts was already the risk-management source for unit 089. Y1 Q3 now
previews the forward link from ρ-limits → Y3 copulas.

**Room made without growing past 120 units:** merged RMT + Ledoit-Wolf into **082**
(one "covariance cleaning" arc + lab) and Fundamental Law + signal combination into
**084** (one Grinold–Kahn arc). Unit 089 and the Q1 checkpoint now require
copula-aware stress of joint losses.

Sources: MFE promoted to ★; Embrechts–McNeil–Straumann 2002 ★; Li 2000 ◆ cautionary;
Nelsen ◆ companion.
