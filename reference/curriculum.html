Expert Curriculum — Quantitative Research

5 years · 200 units · systematic-alpha track + two long-only equity years

A unit ≠ a day. Each row is a concept plus its lab/reproduction plus its quiz. Reading is a small slice of the ~3,350 hours; hands-on labs, validation, and the three capstones are where expertise forms. Years 1–3 are the original QR track (unchanged). Years 4–5 are extra time, not a swap: long-only stocks using ML scores and a constrained optimizer — Year 4 on a weeks-to-months clock, Year 5 on an hours-to-days (mid-frequency) clock. Finish each quarter's checkpoint before advancing. Do not skip to Year 4 or 5 from Year 1.
