Study Roadmap

Reference · the 6-year plan at a glance · print-friendly

Center of gravity: Quantitative Researcher / systematic alpha, then extra years aimed at long-only stock books (ML scores + constrained optimization) — Year 4 on a weeks-to-months clock, Year 5 on an hours-to-days (mid-frequency) clock — then Year 6, the data kit and going-live desk. The one skill that outranks all others: not fooling yourself — leakage-free pipelines, purged/embargoed CV, CPCV, PBO, deflated Sharpe. Years 1–3 are unchanged; Years 4–6 are extra time. Full detail: the expert curriculum. What each later lesson must teach: Year 4 · Year 5 · Year 6. Mandates in plain words: Year 4 · Year 5.

The arc

YearThemeYou can, by the end…
Year 1 Foundations: markets, the math bridge, honest ML Price/hedge an option 3 ways; build the AFML data→label pipeline with no leakage; forecast vol with a validated HAR-RV; explain why a naïve backtest lies.
Year 2 The researcher's craft: ML that survives markets Build a leakage-free, sample-weighted signal model and validate it with CPCV/PBO/deflated Sharpe; reproduce a microstructure result; know when a deep model beats a GBDT.
Year 3 Alpha, execution, systems & the interview Construct a denoised, cost-aware portfolio; model joint losses with copulas (not just correlation); compare Almgren-Chriss vs an RL execution agent; pass the interview gauntlet; defend an end-to-end capstone to a PM.
Year 4 Long-only mid-horizon equity: ML scores, constrained books Build a point-in-time stock universe; beat classic factors (momentum, value, quality) as baselines; train a walk-forward monthly ranker; turn scores into a long-only book with a cost-aware optimizer; defend net information ratio versus a named benchmark.
Year 5 Long-only mid-frequency equity: daily scores, cost-first books Filter by liquidity; beat 1–5 day reversal and PEAD after costs; train a walk-forward daily ranker; turn scores into a long-only book that respects ADV and impact; defend net IR and show why this book's capacity is smaller than Year 4's.
Year 6 Data kit and going live: store, paper loop, tiny live book Build a point-in-time store that both clocks share; run the same functions live as in the backtest; paper-trade with a ledger that adds up and a kill switch that fires; defend twenty paper or tiny-live days.
